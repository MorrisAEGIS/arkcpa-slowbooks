## Design Context

### Users

Ark CPA serves trusted MAGA Energy accounting operators, bookkeepers, and
administrators doing repeat-use financial work. Their core jobs are closing
books, invoicing, receiving payments, paying vendors, running payroll,
reviewing reports, and following the audit trail without losing their place.

### Brand Personality

Trustworthy, sovereign, familiar. The interface should feel controlled and
dependable enough for financial records, unmistakably part of the ARK family,
and immediately legible to someone who already understands SlowBooks or
desktop accounting software.

### Aesthetic Direction

An industrial, refined accounting workbench: a graphite ARK frame around a
calm paper-like working surface. Preserve SlowBooks' compact navigation,
information density, light and dark themes, and practical accounting forms.
Use the canonical Living Ark mark, warm gold for identity and trust, and
restrained mint for primary interaction. Avoid generic SaaS cards, neon
cyan-purple gradients, oversized marketing composition, and decorative
effects inside ledgers and forms.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve accounting muscle memory; modernize the frame before changing
   workflows or information architecture.
2. Keep the current company, primary actions, and financial state visible in
   the first viewport.
3. Use brand color sparingly; semantic accounting states must remain
   conventional, redundant, and accessible.
4. Adapt for mobile instead of shrinking the desktop shell or removing core
   tasks.
5. Treat authentication, reduced motion, keyboard use, privacy, and visible
   provenance as product quality—not finishing work.
